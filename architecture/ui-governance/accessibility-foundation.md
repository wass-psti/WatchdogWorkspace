# Accessibility Foundation — M63

M63 centralizes shared accessibility policy without rewriting feature-specific behavior. Native semantics remain preferred; keyboard focus must be visible; reduced-motion and forced-colors preferences remain first-class; icon-only controls require an accessible name; live regions are available through typed primitives; positive `tabindex` is prohibited in new shared architecture.

Existing shell, Boards, TimeTracker, FuelTrack+, and TradeLink accessibility rules remain compatibility authorities until their controlled migration milestones.
