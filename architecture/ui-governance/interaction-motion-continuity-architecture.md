# Interaction, Motion & Continuity Architecture — M71

M71 consolidates motion policy without replacing the certified v1.30 runtime/orchestrator. Motion is categorized as route, state, overlay, feedback, decorative, or static. Route choreography is scoped to replaceable content; persistent shell chrome remains stable. Reduced-motion handling is mandatory. Transform/opacity are preferred for bounded compositor-friendly motion; permanent `will-change` is prohibited. M66 retains overlay lifecycle ownership and M67 retains feedback semantics. Consumer migration remains M72–M76.
